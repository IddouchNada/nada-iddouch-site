E-commerce

A whole store to rebuild

A complete redesign, from UX to performance tracking.
The goal

Improve the user experience, strengthen organic visibility and support the growth of an industrial B2B site.

How I did it
  1. I led the project and coordinated everyone involved.
  2. I reworked the UX, the site architecture and the conversion funnel.
  3. I took over SEO and content.
  4. I set up performance tracking.
  5. I brought web management in-house.
The result

Organic traffic more than doubled, and a clearer site.

SEO

A brand nobody was searching for

Establishing a little-known brand in France.
The goal

Grow the organic visibility of a brand with little presence in France.

How I did it
  1. I defined the SEO strategy and chose the priority categories.
  2. I analysed search intent and created the content.
  3. I improved internal linking and page structure.
  4. I dealt with cannibalisation between pages.
The result

Better rankings, more impressions and a cleaner structure for what comes next.

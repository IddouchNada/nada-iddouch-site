Case study: redesigning an e-commerce site without losing traffic

A migration to the latest PrestaShop versions and a full redesign of HD Soudage’s e-commerce site. Where a traffic drop of around 20% was expected, traffic grew from 4,000 to 14,000 visits, and the curve keeps rising.

By Nada Iddouch · 25 November 2026 · 4 min read

In short

  • A full redesign with a migration to the latest PrestaShop versions, on an industrial B2B e-commerce site.
  • SEO was built in from the design stage: the expected traffic loss didn’t happen.
  • Traffic grew from 4,000 to 14,000 visits, with healthy, steady progress.

The context

HD Soudage is an industrial B2B company specialising in welding, selling online to professionals. Its e-commerce site ran on an ageing version of PrestaShop. It needed to move to the latest versions of the platform, and the project was the opportunity to rethink the site in depth: user experience, architecture and the buying journey.

The challenge: migrating without breaking SEO

A redesign, and even more so a platform version change, is one of the riskiest moments for organic search. Page templates change, the structure evolves, signals Google knew disappear. On this kind of project, a drop of around 20% of traffic was expected, at least for several months.

My goal was twofold: deliver a more modern, more effective site, without paying that price in traffic.

What I did

  • Project leadership: I ran the redesign end to end and coordinated everyone involved.
  • UX and architecture: I reworked the user experience, the catalogue structure and the conversion funnel, for professional buyers who want to find the right reference fast.
  • SEO and content: I built SEO into the design from the start rather than after launch, and reworked the content following best practices.
  • Measurement: I set up performance tracking to monitor the impact of the migration and steer what came next with reliable data.
  • Bringing it in-house: I brought site management in-house, to stay in control and react fast.

The results

The expected traffic loss never happened. Quite the opposite: since the redesign, traffic has grown from 4,000 to 14,000 visits, and it keeps rising.

What matters most to me is the shape of the curve. It doesn’t shoot up all at once and fall back afterwards: it grows steadily and naturally. That’s the sign of a healthy site, built on solid SEO foundations rather than one-off actions.

What I take away

  • A successful migration is prepared on the SEO side from day one, not at launch.
  • Keeping control of the site in-house means you can fix things fast and don’t have to wait on others.
  • Lasting growth shows in its steadiness: a curve that climbs without jolts beats a spike followed by a drop.

This redesign also laid the groundwork for the next project: building the Spanish site from scratch, in two months.

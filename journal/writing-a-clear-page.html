Writing a page Google understands

A good SEO page is first of all a page that is clear for a human being.

By Nada Iddouch · 9 November 2026 · 2 min read

Key points

  • One page, one topic.
  • The title should say exactly what the person will find.
  • Write to help, not to repeat keywords.

One page, one topic

Each page should answer one question. If two pages on your site say the same thing, they compete with each other. In SEO, this is called cannibalisation.

The title is the shop window

The title tag is often the clickable text in Google’s results. It is the first thing people read. It should be short and precise.

  • Not great: “Home”
  • Better: “Guitar for beginners: how to choose well”

The main heading

The H1 is the big heading you can see on the page. It tells the reader they are in the right place. One clear H1 per page is enough.

The small ad

The meta description is the short text you can see under the title in Google. Its job is to make people want to click. Write it like a small ad. Google can also choose other text from your page.

Really answering

Repeat a keyword twenty times and your text becomes unreadable. Say things simply. Give examples. Add what you know because you have actually done it.

One last question: if Google did not exist, would this page still be useful to someone?

Test yourself

Which title is better for a page about guitars for children: “Our products” or “Kids guitar: which size for which age”?

The second one. It says exactly what the parent will find, in the words they use.

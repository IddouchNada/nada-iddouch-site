My day at FePSeM Day in Lille

Today I’m at FePSeM Day in Lille, a day dedicated to search marketing. Here’s why I’m going and the questions I’m bringing with me.

By Nada Iddouch · 20 November 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • FePSeM Day brings search marketing professionals together in Lille around SEO, SEA, GEO, AI and e-commerce.
  • I’m going to test my self-taught practice against other professionals’ experience.
  • My big questions right now: showing up in AI answers, e-commerce SEO and the right metrics to track.
  • I only keep from a conference what I can test afterwards; a write-up will follow.

Today, off to Lille

This Friday, 20 November, I’m in Lille for FePSeM Day, at Espace Inkermann Châtillon. A full day dedicated to search marketing: talks, case studies and plenty of conversations between practitioners.

FePSeM is the Fédération des Professionnels du Search Marketing, the French federation of search marketing professionals. It brings together people who, like me, spend their days asking how to be found, understood and chosen online. This edition’s programme covers exactly what I’m working on right now: SEO, SEA, GEO, artificial intelligence and e-commerce.

Why I’m going

I learned SEO on my own, in the evenings, watching videos, then building sites and getting a lot wrong. That path gave me good instincts, but it has a limit: you end up going round in circles inside your own certainties.

A day like this is the opposite. You listen to people working on other sites, other industries, other constraints. You compare. You find out what others are testing before it’s written everywhere.

And the timing matters. Search is changing fast: AI-generated answers, conversational engines, where brands fit in those new answers… The job I learned a few years ago is already not quite the same. I’d rather understand that change by talking to the people living it than just put up with it.

The questions I’m bringing

I’m arriving with a few questions in mind, the ones that come up most often in my work:

  • GEO: how can a brand show up in AI answers, and how do you actually measure it?
  • E-commerce: what still works, and what is changing, when it comes to ranking product and category pages?
  • Data: which metrics should you track when some clicks disappear in favour of direct answers?
  • AI day to day: how do you use it to save time without losing quality or a voice of your own?

How I approach a conference

Over time I’ve picked up a few habits. I don’t try to write everything down: I only keep the ideas I can test in the weeks that follow. A conference is only worth it to me if it turns into action.

I also pay as much attention to the breaks as to the talks. That’s often where the most useful exchanges happen: a remark by the coffee machine, a story shared without slides, a new person to follow.

What’s next

In a coming article I’ll share what I take away from the day: the ideas that stuck, the ones I’m going to test, and maybe the ones I disagree with.

And if you’re at FePSeM Day today too, come and say hello or message me on LinkedIn. I’ve always found the best ideas come out of conversations.

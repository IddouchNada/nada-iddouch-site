Measure, then start again

SEO is never finished. You look at what works, you correct, and you start again.

By Nada Iddouch · 9 November 2026 · 2 min read

Key points

  • Search Console is free and essential.
  • Four numbers are enough to begin.
  • Change one thing at a time, then measure.

Your free dashboard

Search Console is a free tool from Google. It shows how your site appears in the results. If you install only one tool, make it this one.

The four numbers to understand

  • Impressions: how many times your site appeared in the results.
  • Clicks: how many times someone clicked.
  • CTR: clicks divided by impressions. This is the click-through rate.
  • Average position: your average place in the results.

Reading the numbers like a story

  • Lots of impressions, few clicks: your title is not appealing. Rework it.
  • A position around 8 to 15: you are almost at the top. Improve the page and add internal links.
  • Clicks that suddenly drop: look for what changed. A broken page, an update, a competitor.

Be patient

Google says it itself: a change can show in a few hours, or take several months. Change one thing at a time. Write down the date. Look at the result.

That is SEO: you test, you get it wrong, you start again.

Test yourself

A page has many impressions but almost no clicks. What do you look at first?

The title and the description. Your page is seen, but it does not make people want to click.

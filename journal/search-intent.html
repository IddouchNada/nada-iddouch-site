What people really want when they search

The words typed into Google are only the surface. The real subject is what the person wants to do.

By Nada Iddouch · 9 November 2026 · 2 min read

Key points

  • Behind every search, there is a need.
  • Google’s results show you that need.
  • A page should answer one need, clearly.

Same topic, different needs

Look at these four searches. They are all about guitars. Yet nobody wants the same thing.

  • “how to tune a guitar”: the person wants to learn.
  • “best guitar for beginners”: they are comparing before buying.
  • “buy cheap acoustic guitar”: they are ready to pay.
  • “fender official site”: they want to go somewhere specific.

This need hidden behind the words is called search intent. It is probably the most important idea in SEO.

How to recognise it

The easiest way: type the search into Google and look at the first page.

  • Articles? People want to learn.
  • Comparisons? They are hesitating.
  • Product pages? They want to buy.

Google has already watched what people prefer. It shows you the answer for free.

The most common mistake

Many beginners push a product page on a search where people want to learn. The page can be perfect. It just does not answer the right question.

Before writing, ask yourself one question: what does this person want to do, right now?

Test yourself

Someone searches for “how to change guitar strings”. What do you offer them: a product page or a tutorial?

A tutorial. The person wants to learn. You can suggest strings later in the article.

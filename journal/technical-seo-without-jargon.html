Technical SEO, without the jargon

Technical SEO is the plumbing of a website. When everything works, nobody sees it. When it breaks, everybody feels it.

By Nada Iddouch · 9 November 2026 · 2 min read

Key points

  • Google uses the mobile version of your site.
  • A fast page keeps its visitors better.
  • A page that changes address should always be redirected.

Mobile first

Google uses the mobile version of your site to understand and rank it. If something is missing on mobile, it may be missing for Google too.

Speed

Nobody likes waiting. On a slow page, people leave. Start with the simplest thing: lighter images. The free tool PageSpeed Insights will tell you what to fix.

The sitemap: the list of your pages

A sitemap is a file that gives Google the list of pages you want to show. It helps Google find them. It does not guarantee they will be filed.

Robots.txt: the closed doors

This file tells robots where not to go. Be careful: it is not a way to hide a page from Google. For that, you use a noindex tag.

The redirect: the change of address

When a page changes address, a permanent redirect, called a 301, sends visitors and Google to the new one. Without it, you lose the work done on the old page. It is the number one rule of any redesign.

The canonical: the official version

Sometimes several addresses show the same content. The canonical tag tells Google which one is the right one.

Test yourself

You change the address of a page that performs well. What must you not forget?

A 301 redirect from the old address to the new one.

Hobbies

Beyond the CV: music, mountains and stars.

A resume shows what I do. This page is closer to who I am — three things I care about, and the stories behind why they stuck.

What drives me

Music, or how it started with a disagreement with my father

One evening, my father was watching Pink Floyd's "Echoes" live at Pompeii on YouTube. I told him it sounded noisy. He showed me who Pink Floyd really were — and my love for music has only grown since. Today it's become essential to me. I wanted to learn guitar; my parents got me one, and I taught myself entirely through YouTube videos, letting my imagination run free. That experience taught me something I still rely on: I can learn anything, as long as I'm passionate about it. So for everything I struggle with, I try to learn to love it, to find interest and stay passionate — I need to give meaning to what I do and feel like I'm learning something new.

Reconnecting

Hiking, to find my proper scale

I love hiking because it lets me reconnect with nature and feel my own scale. Standing in front of something that vast, the ego takes a hit — in a good way.

Looking up

Astronomy, from a locked-down sky

It started with the movie Interstellar, then during lockdown, watching the stars every evening. I ended up buying a telescope. Today, when I travel, I like getting to know the sky wherever I am — much like I do with hiking or food: discovering new places and cultures, in my own way.
